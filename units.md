# 2. Units and membership

**2.1. Unit definition.** A unit is an organized group within Orbio.

## 2.2. Unit levels and names

| Unit | Maximum members | Lead title |
|---|---:|---|
| Circle | 10 | Lead |
| House | 100 | Lead |
| Commons | 1,000 | Lead |
| Town | 10,000 | Mayor |
| City | 100,000 | Mayor |
| Province | 1,000,000 | Governor |
| Region | 10,000,000 | Governor |
| Country | 100,000,000 | President |
| Federation | 1,000,000,000 | President |
| Alliance | 10,000,000,000 | President |
| World | 100,000,000,000 | President |

**2.3. Location.** Units form voluntarily, online or in person. Each unit decides whether to organize around a physical location.

**2.4. Unit mission.** Each unit must have a specific mission that contributes to Orbio’s overall mission.

## 2.5. Individual membership

**Joining.** Every member must belong to at least one Circle. Individuals may join additional Circles. They belong to larger units only through their Circles’ affiliations.

**Joining another Circle.** Members should join an additional Circle only if they have time for its weekly meetings and can meet its [support commitments](circles.md#44-practical-support).

**Leaving.** A member may leave a Circle at any time. Leaving or being expelled from their last Circle ends their Orbio membership until they join an Orbio Circle again.

## 2.6. Unit admission and affiliation

**Admission.** A unit joins Orbio when the Overall Community Lead approves its admission or when it joins an existing Orbio unit exactly one level higher.

**Affiliation.** Once admitted, a unit may remain independent or belong to one or more larger units. It must meet each larger unit’s written conditions of membership.

### 2.6.1. Growth into a larger unit

If admitting an individual or a member unit would exceed its population limit, a unit may reorganize under its own rules at the lowest higher level that can accommodate the new population.

The unit retains its Orbio membership and may form the smaller units it needs without the Overall Community Lead’s approval. Every individual must continue to belong to at least one Circle.

## 2.7. Population counts

Each unit counts each individual once, even if they belong through several member units. For example, someone in two Circles within the same House counts once in each Circle and once in the House.
