# 5. Community governance

## 5.1. Community Council

**Seats.** Every unit that belongs to no larger unit has a Council seat.

**Representation.** Each member chooses one chain of affiliated units to represent them. The chain starts with one of their Circles and ends at a unit with a Council seat. Only units in that chain may count the member toward their voting weight.

**Council votes.** The Lead of the unit at the end of the chain casts one vote for each member the unit represents.

## 5.2. Overall Community Lead

The Overall Community Lead serves all of Orbio. The Community may include multiple Worlds, each with its own President.

### 5.2.1. Community Vice-Lead

Before Orbio begins operating, the first Overall Community Lead must appoint a Community Vice-Lead. Each incoming Lead must appoint a Vice-Lead upon taking office and promptly fill any vacancy in that role.

### 5.2.2. Temporary incapacity

The Vice-Lead becomes Acting Overall Community Lead in either of the following circumstances.

- The Overall Community Lead declares that they are temporarily unable to serve.
- At least **80% of the Council’s total voting weight** confirms the Lead’s incapacity.

The Acting Lead exercises all powers of the office, including [approval of amendments](#54-amendments), until the Overall Community Lead can resume their duties.

### 5.2.3. Vacancy in office

If the Overall Community Lead dies, resigns, or is removed, the Vice-Lead immediately becomes Acting Overall Community Lead and exercises all powers of the office.

The Council must elect a successor within **60 days** of the vacancy, using the voting weights in [§5.1](#51-community-council). The successor takes office upon election.

### 5.2.4. Removal for misconduct

**Grounds.** The Council may remove the Overall Community Lead for serious misconduct. Examples include fraud, misuse of Community resources, abuse of authority, and serious or repeated violations of this charter.

**Hearing.** Before voting, the Council must give the Lead the allegations in writing and a reasonable opportunity to respond.

**Vote.** If the Lead represents a Council unit, that unit chooses another member to cast its votes. Removal takes effect when at least **80% of the Council’s total voting weight** supports it. The Council must record its reasons in writing.

## 5.3. Elections

The Council elects the Overall Community Lead every **five years** and holds a special election when the [vacancy rule](#523-vacancy-in-office) requires one. Every election uses the Council voting weights in [§5.1](#51-community-council).

Before nominations open, the Council must publish the threshold for election and the procedure for resolving ties. Those rules apply throughout that election.

## 5.4. Amendments

An amendment to the Community rules requires both approvals.

- Support from at least **80% of the Council’s total voting weight**.
- Approval from the Overall Community Lead.
