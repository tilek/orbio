# Orbio · Weekly Circle Meeting

**Led by the Circle Lead.** 

## 1. Welcome · ~10 minutes

Greet everyone by name. 
After the first meeting, revisit the action the Circle chose last week. What happened? Did it help?

## 2. Member round · ~10 minutes per person

Give each member a turn, including the Lead. Start by listening. If someone is having a difficult week, make room for emotional support and ask what would help. Use these questions to guide the conversation.

| Topic | What to ask |
|---|---|
| Check-in | How are you feeling? How have things been for you since we last met? |
| Mission | What did you do this week to help our Circle’s mission? |
| Helping others | Did you get a chance to help anyone in the Circle? |
| Asking for help | What help would make a difference to you this coming week? |
| Gratitude | What are you grateful for this week? |
| Appreciation | Is there someone in the Circle you’d like to thank? What would you like them to know? |

## 3. Wrap-up · ~10 minutes

- Summarize what members asked for and what others offered. Confirm who will follow up on each offer.
- Choose one concrete action together to help a member or the wider world. Agree on who will take it forward and revisit it at the next meeting.
- Thank everyone and close the meeting.

For the Circle’s commitments on emotional support, gratitude, practical help, and weekly action, see [Life in a Circle](circles.md).
