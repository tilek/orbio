# Orbio

Orbio is designed to become a multiplanetary community.

Our mission is to help life across universe flourish, now and forever.

## Inspiration

Orbio is inspired by humans' collaboration and accomplishments within religions such as Christianity and Islam as well as countries like the United States of America.

## How it works

Every member belongs to one or more Community Units (Units).  
  
Units are hierarchical and nested.   
Each Unit size level has a [dedicated name](2.units.md).  
Each Unit must have its own mission, rules, a Lead, and a Vice-Lead.  
Each Unit mission must contribute to a larger unit's mission and Community's overall mission.  
  
The smallest Unit is a Circle.  
  
Circle contains up to 10 people.  
Circle members meet weekly.  
During the meetings they check-in with each other, figure out how they can support each other and their Circle mission overall and set plans for next week.
